# Holtzman 0.7.1 (pre-release)

Installs over 0.7.0 (same signing key). Fixes found while trying 0.7.0 on a Pixel 10a.

**What changed since 0.7.0**
- **The fade when the camera reopens at 1x no longer flashes black twice.** It lifted when the camera session was ready, before the first preview frame, so the picture went black again; it now lifts with the first frame. Measured on the phone: one dark period, then a smooth fade in.
- **A shot at 0.6x no longer says two things.** It said both "Ultra HDR needs 1×" and "ultrawide, no merge"; zoomed shots now say only the zoom note ("2.8× crop", "ultrawide, no merge").
- **Settings, About:** the denoiser row said "Learned, on the TPU" although that denoiser has been off by default since 2026-11-02; it now reads "Learned denoiser: Available, off", and the Computational photo subtitle no longer claims TPU denoising. The model credits (DeepLab v3, MiDaS) were cut off and now fit.

**Seen working on the phone (0.7.0 and this build):** the shutter dip, the thumbnail spinner with "Saving…", the first-run card closing on a tap, sliding along the zoom numbers (2.8×), "Ultra HDR needs 1×" when zoomed, "Saved · 2.8× crop", the .6x switch and its fade, the Settings developer section appearing on the seventh tap of Version and not the sixth.

**Not tried:** longer failure messages (no failure to trigger), video, the front camera, the timer, Styles, landscape, large font sizes, faces, pets, low light and outdoor scenes.
