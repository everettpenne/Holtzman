# Holtzman 0.8.1 (pre-release)

Polish for the Liquid Glass experiment (Settings > Camera > Liquid Glass (experiment), off by default). With it off, nothing changes from 0.7.1.

- The light rim and colour fringe on the edges of glass buttons and panels are gone; the edge only bends the picture. The shutter ring and the review button lose their white outlines in glass mode.
- The bars above and below the picture no longer show a stretched, zoomed-in copy of the frame, then a blocky one; they show a smooth blurred wash of the scene's colours.
- Glass buttons keep their press swell.

Checked on an emulator only (launch and main screen); unit tests pass. Not yet seen on the Pixel 10a: the real look, frame rate and heat.
