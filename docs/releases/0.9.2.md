# Holtzman 0.9.2 (pre-release)

Better integration with gallery apps.

## In-app viewer
- Tap the round review button beside the shutter to swipe through what Holtzman saved (photos, or videos in video mode), newest first. Pinch or double-tap to zoom a photo; a tap hides the controls; Back closes it.
- A share button sends the photo (the JPEG, when a DNG was saved with it) or video to any app. An "open in gallery" button hands it to your gallery app.
- Videos show their first frame with a play button that hands the video to a player (the viewer does not play video itself). Photos are shown at up to about 2400 px on the long side, without Ultra HDR brightness. No delete in the viewer.

## Other apps can start Holtzman
- Android's own camera requests now reach Holtzman: STILL_IMAGE_CAMERA and VIDEO_CAMERA open the camera (video mode for the second); IMAGE_CAPTURE and VIDEO_CAPTURE take one shot and return it, copied to the output the caller named, or as the file's address with read permission. The shot stays in the gallery either way.
- The last video shows in the review button when the app opens (as the last photo already did), and videos now carry their date taken.
- If no gallery app can open a shot, the status says so; long-press on the review button opens Sietch, or the system's photo list when Sietch is not installed.

## What was and was not checked
- Emulator only: the viewer (open, swipe, hide controls, double-tap zoom, pan while zoomed, the share sheet appearing), the video-camera intent opening video mode, an image-capture request taking a shot and closing; unit tests pass.
- Not tried: the viewer on the Pixel 10a, the video page, copying a shot to a caller's output, a real gallery app calling Holtzman.
- 0.9.1's controls and 0.9.0's aspect ratios are unchanged.
