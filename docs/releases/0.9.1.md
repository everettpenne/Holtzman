# Holtzman 0.9.1 (pre-release)

The Liquid Glass experiment is over: the glass is gone and the controls are solid cards.

- The glass was blotchy and jittery (it was drawn from a small copy of the viewfinder, read back several times a second) and cost rendering time on every frame. A neural accelerator cannot render it, so it is removed, together with that read-back.
- The controls keep the iOS 26 shapes: round buttons that swell under the finger, rounded dark cards, a sheet that opens out of its button.
- Settings > Camera: the switch is now "iOS 26 style controls". It is on by default (the 0.8.x notes said the glass was off by default; it was on). Off gives the flat look of 0.7.1.
- The aspect ratio viewfinder of 0.9.0 is unchanged.

Checked on an emulator only (4:3 and 16:9, the controls sheet, the aspect card; no crash); unit tests pass. Not yet seen on the Pixel 10a.
