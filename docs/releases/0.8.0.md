# Holtzman 0.8.0 (pre-release)

An experiment: a Liquid Glass look for the camera controls, styled after the iOS 26 Camera. It is **off by default**; turn it on in Settings > Camera > Liquid Glass (experiment). With it off, the app looks and behaves as in 0.7.1.

## What the experiment does
- Controls sheet, status pill, first-run tip, zoom bubble, round buttons and the shutter ring become frosted, light-bending glass over the live picture.
- Glass is used only for the control layer, never on content, and never glass on glass (the sheet's tiles are flat).
- Falls back to plain surfaces when the system's Reduce Transparency is on, or in the screen-calm mode.
- Details: docs/liquid-glass.md.

## What was and was not checked
- Built and smoke-tested (launch, open the controls sheet, no crash) on an emulator; unit tests pass.
- **Not yet seen on the Pixel 10a**: the real look, frame rate and heat (the glass samples a small copy of the viewfinder about 10 times a second), the press swell, the flash/night buttons, the readout, the dial cards with real content, real-finger gestures.
- If it looks or runs badly on the phone, leave the setting off.
