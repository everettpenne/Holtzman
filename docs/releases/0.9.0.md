# Holtzman 0.9.0 (pre-release)

## Aspect ratio sets the viewfinder
- Pick 4:3 (default, the sensor's own shape), 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9 in the controls sheet (Aspect). The viewfinder takes that shape and shows the picture cut as the saved photo will be.
- 16:9 is taller than 4:3 and runs down under the controls; 1:1 and 4:5 are shorter, with black above and below.
- The controls (zoom numbers, status, shutter row, sheet) stay where they were, on top of the picture. Tap to focus follows the cropped picture.
- The viewfinder is cropped only where the saved photo is: merged photos with RAW available. Video, below 1x and the front camera (the camera's own JPEG) stay 4:3.
- The "Full (4:3)" label is now "4:3".

## Liquid Glass (experiment, off by default)
- Glass samples only the visible picture and uses a smaller snapshot refreshed about 8 times a second, to cost less per frame.
- Earlier in this line (0.8.x): no rim light on glass edges, plain black bars above and below the picture.

## What was and was not checked
- Emulator only: 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9 viewfinders with the aspect card and the controls sheet; unit tests pass.
- Not yet seen on the Pixel 10a: the real look, the glass frame rate and heat, subject-tracking focus on cropped ratios (it uses the whole sensor frame, so it may aim a little off the visible crop), 1:1 and 4:5 with glass controls.
