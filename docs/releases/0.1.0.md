# Holtzman 0.1.0 (pre-release)

A camera for the Glass suite, built for the Google Pixel 10a. This is a new app, not the GrapheneOS Camera fork in [HoltzmanCamera](https://github.com/everettpenne/HoltzmanCamera); it installs beside that one (package `com.everettpenne.holtzman`).

**What it does:** Camera2 photo and video, RAW/DNG, Night (Google's extension), Ultra HDR, and a burst mode that aligns and merges six RAW frames on the GPU (about 1.6 s from shutter to saved), then renders them with a lens-shading-corrected demosaic, a Pixel-like tone and colour finish, and a noise-aware denoise and sharpen. A **DATA** chip saves raw frames plus metadata to `Documents/Holtzman/Dataset` for tuning.

**Read before installing**
- Tested on one phone: a Pixel 10a on GrapheneOS (Android 17). Nothing else has been tried.
- The **noise-aware denoise and sharpen** (the newest part) was verified on an emulator against the CPU renderer, **not yet on the phone**. If its shaders do not build on a device, the app falls back to a simpler sharpen pass; it does not crash.
- The Tensor NPU is not used: Google's Edge TPU service refuses on-device compilation for third-party apps (see docs/ix-pipeline.md). Everything runs on the CPU and GPU.
- Tuned against the Pixel camera app on a single dim indoor scene. Bright and outdoor scenes are unverified.
- Pre-release: expect rough edges.
