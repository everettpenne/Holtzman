# Holtzman 0.7.0 (pre-release)

Installs over 0.6.2 (same signing key). The controls and feedback were reworked after a review of the app as a UX engineer would do it.

**What changed since 0.6.2**
- **Controls sheet rebuilt.** One solid surface (it was two shades, with the zoom numbers ghosting through), the picture behind it dimmed, buttons in sections (Shoot, Look, Manual, More) four across, no clipped names, Back closes it instead of leaving the app, ISO shows its name instead of sharing Exposure's icon, higher-contrast labels.
- **Feedback.** A dip to black over the picture when the shutter is pressed; the thumbnail dims and spins while a photo is taken or processed; failures stay on screen for 7 s (confirmations 3.5 s); a short veil while the camera reopens on crossing 1x.
- **Zoom.** Slide along the zoom numbers to zoom continuously (taps still jump to a number); the numbers are 48 dp touch targets with spoken descriptions. Saved photos say when they were zoomed ("4.0× crop") or ultrawide ("no merge"); HDR and RAW say why they are unavailable when zoomed or below 1x.
- **Quieter by default.** The ISO / shutter / EV / focus readout is off until switched on (Settings > Capture and guides, or the photo settings sheet). A first-run card says what the touches do (tap to focus, hold to lock, pinch or slide to zoom, swipe for video, six dots for controls).
- **Settings.** The dataset tool, the raw-burst switches and the comparison tools are hidden until the Version row is tapped seven times (tap seven times again to hide them). The About section credits the two on-device models (DeepLab v3, Apache-2.0; MiDaS v2.1 small, MIT) and states that the app has no network permission.

**Read before installing**
- **The phone was disconnected before most of this was tried on it.** On a Pixel 10a (earlier today) I saw the new sheet, Back closing it, the .6x and 4x fixes of 0.6.2. On the Android emulator I saw the first-run card (and its tap to close), the readout being off, the thumbnail spinner, the sheet and a save in the release build. **Not seen working on the phone:** the shutter dip, the reopen veil, sliding along the zoom numbers, the 48 dp zoom targets, the longer failure messages, the Settings changes and the HDR/RAW explanations. Not tried anywhere: video, the front camera, the timer, Styles, landscape, large font sizes.
- Still open from the review: the saved zoomed photo is cropped sensor pixels, not enlarged (4x is about 0.75 MP); there is no "reset all settings"; the top-bar icons are unchanged (yellow means on).
- Dataset mode is now behind the seven taps; shots in it still cannot go below 1x.
