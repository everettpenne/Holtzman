# Holtzman 0.9.4 (pre-release)

## Lock-screen camera
- The lock-screen camera is now a separate screen, opened only by the power button's double press or the lock screen's camera button. The normal app no longer shows over the lock screen.
- While locked it shows no thumbnail of older photos and no Settings. The viewer holds only the shots from that session, with Delete only (no Share, no "open in gallery", no video playback).
- It closes as soon as the screen goes off, from the power button or the timeout. The next wake shows the lock screen, not the camera. Before, it could stay open behind the dark screen, using battery, and come back over the lock screen on every wake.
- On the lock screen the screen stays on for 30 s after the last touch. There is no first-run tip there.

## Camera
- The screen stays on while the camera is in use, and goes back to the phone's own timeout after two minutes without a touch. Before, it dimmed and slept while you framed a shot.
- Going from .6× straight to 2× (or any zoom) lands on that zoom. Before, the camera reopened at 1×.
- Debug switches from the launch intent are taken only by debug builds.

## Viewer
- The delete dialog matches the app: dark card, Delete in red.

## What was and was not checked
- On the Pixel 10a (debug build): the locked restrictions, keeping the screen on, zoom .6×/1×/2×/8×, 16:9 and 1:1 saves at the right size, the HDR badge, viewer swipe and delete, video recording, the front camera. Unit tests pass.
- Closing on screen off: emulator only. Not yet checked on the phone with the real double press.
- Not checked: the older-photo thumbnail, Share and "open in gallery", Settings, and another app asking Holtzman for a photo. These need the phone unlocked.
