# Holtzman 0.9.3 (pre-release)

## Focus follows the picture
- With a ratio narrower than the sensor's (1:1, 4:5, 16:9), subject and face tracking ignore anything outside what the picture shows. Before, a person just outside the frame could take focus from the subject in the photo. (The camera's own built-in face autofocus is not covered.)

## Viewer
- Delete: a trash button asks "Delete this photo?" (or video) and removes it, with the DNG saved beside it.
- HDR: the window goes into HDR colour mode while the viewer is open (where the screen supports it), so Ultra HDR photos are shown with their extra brightness, marked "HDR".

## Lock-screen camera
- Holtzman answers the lock screen's camera request (the lock-screen button, the power button's double press) and can show over the lock screen. While locked it shows no thumbnail of older photos, the viewer holds only what was shot in that session, and sharing and "open in gallery" are hidden.
- GrapheneOS ships its own Camera app; whether the system picks Holtzman for these requests, or needs it chosen as the default somewhere, is not known.

## What was and was not checked
- Emulator only: delete (5 photos to 4), the locked behaviour (forced with a debug flag; the emulator has no lock set), unit tests pass.
- Not checked: HDR rendering (the emulator's photos have no gain map and its display cannot show HDR), a real lock-screen launch, any of it on the Pixel 10a or GrapheneOS.
