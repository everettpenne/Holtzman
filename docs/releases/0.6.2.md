# Holtzman 0.6.2 (pre-release)

Installs over 0.6.1 (same signing key).

**What changed since 0.6.1**
- **Zoom now reaches the saved photo.** Zooming in changed the viewfinder but the photo came out at 1x. Cause: the photos are built from RAW frames, and the RAW stream is never cropped by the zoom ratio. The finished picture is now cut to the middle part the viewfinder showed (4x gives a 750 x 1000 photo: real sensor pixels, not enlarged). A zoomed photo carries no Ultra HDR gain map, as with the Ratio crop.
- **0.6x really uses the ultrawide.** With a RAW stream open, the camera stayed on the main lens below 1x, so 0.6x showed and saved the 1x view. Below 1x the camera now reopens without RAW (and with it again at 1x and above); the shot there is the camera's own JPEG, without Holtzman's merge and tone processing.

**Read before installing**
- Checked on one Pixel 10a: 1x, 4x and 0.6x each match their viewfinder. Not checked: pinching across 1x by hand (the camera reopens there, with a brief flicker, and lands on 1x), 2x and 8x, the front camera.
- Dataset mode keeps RAW all the time, so it cannot shoot below 1x.
